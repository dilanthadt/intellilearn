The State of the Art of AI  
• Based on One Hundred Year Study on AI 2018 and 2019.  
• Publications: AI papers increased 20-fold between 2010 and 2019 to  
  about 20,000 a year. The most popular category was machine learning.  
  
• Students: Course enrollment increased 5-fold in the U.S. and 16-fold  
  internationally from a 2010 baseline. AI is the most popular  
  specialization in Computer Science.  
  
• Conferences: Attendance at NeurIPS increased 800% since 2012 to  
  13,500 attendees. Other conferences are seeing annual growth of  
  about 30%.  
  
• Industry: AI startups in the U.S. increased 20-fold to over 800.
