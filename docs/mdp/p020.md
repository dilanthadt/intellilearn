Discounting rewards  
▪     How to discount?  
      ▪     Each time we descend a level, we multiply in the discount  
        once                                                        1  
▪     Why discount?  
      ▪     Sooner rewards probably do have higher utility than later  
        rewards  
      ▪     Also helps our algorithms converge  
▪     Example: discount of 0.5                                          γ  
      ▪     U([1,2,3]) = 1×1 + 0.5×2 + 0.25×3  
      ▪     U([1,2,3]) \< U([3,2,1])
