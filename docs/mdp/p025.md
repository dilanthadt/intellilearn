Discounting example: Quiz 1 (γ = 1)                                                                                   ADDED SLIDE  
  
  
  
  
With γ = 1 there is no penalty for waiting, so only the size of the exit reward matters.  
  
  
    State              Go West, then Exit at a          Go East, then Exit at e    Best        Optimal policy  
  
       b                          γ1 × 10 = 10                   γ3 × 1 = 1        West        Exit at a and e. From b, c and d  
        always go West towards the 10.  
        c                         γ2 × 10 = 10                   γ2 × 1 = 1        West  
  
       d                          γ3 × 10 = 10                   γ1 × 1 = 1        West  
  
The exit reward is received after n moves, so it is discounted by γn.  
  
  
Optimal policy and values V\*(s)  
        Exit       ←                 ←    ←      Exit  
  
        10         10                10   10      1  
        a          b                c    d       e
