### L12 – Markov Decision Processes — page 14

MDP DEFINITIONS  
  
  
  
Why is it Markov?  
▪     “Markov” generally means that given the present state, the future and the past are  
      independent  
▪     For Markov decision processes, “Markov” means action outcomes depend only on  
      the current state  
  
  
  
  
        P(St+1 = s′ \| St = st, At = at, St−1 = st−1, At−1, … , S0 = s0)  
        = P(St+1 = s′ \| St = st, At = at)  
  
  
        Andrey Markov (1856 to 1922)  
▪     This is just like search, where the successor function could only depend on the  
      current state (not the history)
