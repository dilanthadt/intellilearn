### Bayesian Network Self-Study Tutorial — page 1

        Answering Queries with a Bayesian Network  
        A Self Study Tutorial using the Burglary Alarm Example  
   CS3613 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, University of  
        Moratuwa  
  
  
1. What you will learn  
This tutorial walks you through one small Bayesian network and shows how it can answer several  
different kinds of probabilistic questions. Every calculation is worked out step by step so that you can  
follow along with a calculator. By the end you should be able to:  
    •   Read a Bayesian network and its conditional probability tables (CPTs).  
    •   Write down the joint probability of any complete assignment using the chain rule.  
    •   Compute marginal, predictive (causal), diagnostic and intercausal queries by enumeration.  
    •   Explain why a Bayesian network is far more economical than a full joint probability table.  
    •   Recognize the phenomenon called explaining away.  
  
  
2. The network  
You are away from home. You have installed a burglar alarm that is fairly reliable at detecting a  
burglary, but it also occasionally goes off during a minor earthquake. Two neighbours, John and Mary,  
have promised to phone you if they hear the alarm. John sometimes confuses the telephone ringing  
with the alarm, and Mary likes loud music and sometimes misses the alarm altogether.
