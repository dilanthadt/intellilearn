### Knowledge Representation — page 5

Categories and Objects  
    We organize objects into categories  
    Most of the reasoning takes place at the level of  
    categories  
  
    Agents can  
      Infer the presence of objects from perceptual input  
      Infer the category membership from the perceived properties  
      Use that category information to make predictions about the  
      objects
