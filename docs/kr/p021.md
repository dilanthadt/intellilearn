Processes  
 AKA liquid events  
 Different from discrete events  
 Analogous to the difference between substances and  
 objects  
 Any subinterval of a process is also a member of the  
 same process category  
     (e ∈ Processes) ∧ Happens(e, (t1, t4)) ∧ (t1 \< t2 \< t3 \< t4)  
     ⇒ Happens(e, (t2, t3))
