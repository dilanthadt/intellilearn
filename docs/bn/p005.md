### L11 – Bayesian Networks — page 5

Example  
   Burglar alarm at home  
     Turns on if a burglary has occurred.  
     Fairly reliable at detecting a burglary   but responds at times,  
      to minor earthquakes as well.  
  
  
 Two neighbors John and Mary may call the  
    police on hearing alarm.  
     John always calls when he hears the alarm, but sometimes  
      confuses the telephone ringing with the alarm and calls then,  
      too.  
     Mary likes loud music and sometimes misses the alarm  
      altogether
