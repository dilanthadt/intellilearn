Another example  
  
• Given that the grass is wet, what is the probability  
  that it has rained?  
  
        P(r, w)  
        P(r \| w) =         ∝ ∑ P(c, s, r, w)  
        P(w) C=c,S=s  
        = ∑ P(c)P(s \| c)P(r \| c)P(w \| r, s)  
        C=c,S=s  
  
        = ∑ P(c)P(r \| c)∑ P(w \| r, s)P(s \| c)  
        C=c             S=s  
  
        = ∑ P(c)P(r \| c)P(w \| r, c)  
        C=c  
  
        = P(w \| r)∑ P(r \| c)P(c)  
        C=c  
  
        = P(w \| r)P(r)
