### L11 – Bayesian Networks — page 6

Example  
 Variables: Burglary, Earthquake, Alarm, JohnCalls, MaryCalls  
  
  
 Network topology reflects "causal" knowledge:  
   A burglar can turn the alarm on  
   An earthquake can turn the alarm on  
   The alarm can cause Mary to call  
   The alarm can cause John to call  
  
  
 Deal with questions like: neighbor John calls, but neighbor  
  Mary doesn't call. Is there a burglar?
