### L11 – Bayesian Networks — page 13

Conditional independence relationships  
  
  
• Suppose the alarm went off. Does knowing whether there was a  
  burglary change the probability of John calling?  
   P(John \| Alarm, Burglary) = P(John \| Alarm)  
• Suppose the alarm went off. Does knowing whether John called  
  change the probability of Mary calling?  
   P(Mary \| Alarm, John) = P(Mary \| Alarm)
