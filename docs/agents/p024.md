### Lecture 2 – Intelligent Agents — page 24

Properties of Task Environments  
• Dynamic Vs. Static  
  • Whether the environment can change while the agent’s deliberating or  
    not.  
  • E.g., Taxi driving – Dynamic, Crossword puzzles – Static  
  
• Discrete Vs. Continuous  
  • Apply to the state of the environment, the way time is handled, and the  
    percepts and actions of the agent.  
  • E.g.,  
     • Taxi driving is a continuous state and continuous-time problem.  
     • Taxi-driving actions are also continuous.  
     • Chess: Fixed number of moves
