### Lecture 2 – Intelligent Agents — page 22

Properties of Task Environments  
• Deterministic vs. Nondeterministic vs. Stochastic  
  •   Whether the next state of the environment is completely determined  
      by the current state and the action executed by the agent(s) or not.  
  •   Most real situations are not deterministic.  
      • E.g., Taxi driving  
  •   Stochastic – When the environment explicitly deals with  
      probabilities.  
      • There’s a 25% chance of rain tomorrow
