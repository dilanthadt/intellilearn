### Lecture 2 – Intelligent Agents — page 37

Goal-based Agents  
• Combines prior knowledge and perceptions and takes actions  
  to achieve a goal.  
• E.g., Robot car  
   • Goal: Customer Destination (Say Ratmalana)  
   • Actions: at a junction, turn left/right/go straight  
• More flexible than reflex agents  
   • Changing the goal will change the actions appropriately. E.g., Robot  
     Taxi driver
