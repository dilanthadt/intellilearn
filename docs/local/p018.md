Variants of Hill Climbing  
• Stochastic hill climbing  
  • Chooses at random from among the uphill moves; the probability of  
    selection can vary with the steepness of the uphill move.  
  • This usually converges more slowly than steepest ascent, but in some  
    state landscapes, it finds better solutions.  
  
  
• First-choice hill climbing  
  • Implements stochastic hill climbing by generating successors randomly  
    until one is generated that is better than the current state.  
  • This is a good strategy when a state has many (e.g., thousands) of  
    successors.
