### L04 – Local Search & Optimization — page 20

Simulated Annealing  
• A hill-climbing algorithm that never makes “downhill” moves is always  
  vulnerable to getting stuck in a local maximum.  
• A purely random walk that moves to a successor state without  
  concern for the value will eventually discover the global maximum  
  but will be extremely inefficient.  
• Combine the ideas: add some randomness to hill-climbing to allow  
  the possibility of escape from a local optimum.  
  
• Simulated annealing wanders around during the early parts of the  
  search, hopefully toward a good general region of the state space  
• Toward the end, the algorithm does a more focused search, making  
  a few bad moves.
