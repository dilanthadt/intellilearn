### L04 – Local Search & Optimization — page 25

Local Beam Search  
• Keep track of k states rather than  
  just one, as in hill climbing  
  
• Begins with k randomly generated  
  states  
• At each step, all successors of all k  
  states are generated  
• If anyone is a goal, algorithm halts  
• Otherwise, selects the best k  
  successors from the complete list,  
  and repeats
