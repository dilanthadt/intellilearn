### L04 – Local Search & Optimization — page 17

Hill Climbing Search Problems  
• Ridges: It is a region that is higher than its neighbors but itself  
  has a slope.  
   • It is a special kind of local maximum.  
   • Results in a sequence of local maxima that is very difficult for greedy  
     algorithms to navigate.  
• Plateau: the evaluation function is flat, resulting in a random  
  walk.
