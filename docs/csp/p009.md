### L05 – Constraint Satisfaction Problems — page 9

Why Formulate a Problem as a CSP?  
• Provide a natural representation for a wide variety of  
  problems.  
• CPS solvers are fast and efficient.  
• Can quickly eliminate a large portion of the search  
  space that violates the constraints which an atomic  
  state-space searcher cannot.  
   • E.g., Once we have chosen SA = blue in the Australia  
    problem, we can conclude that none of the five neighboring  
    variables can take on the value.
