### L13 – Reinforcement Learning — page 46

ONE THING TD ON V CANNOT DO  
  
To choose actions from V, you need the model again  
  
  From V: needs a look ahead  
  
        π(s) = argmaxa Σs′ T(s, a, s′) [ R(s, a, s′) + γ V(s′) ]  
  
  
  
  From Q: just pick the best  
  
        π(s) = argmaxa Q(s, a)  
  
        So learn Q values, not V values. Then acting needs no model at all.
