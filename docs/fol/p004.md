### First-Order Logic — page 4

Ontological Commitment  
    What the language assumes about the nature of reality  
    FOL vs Propositional logic  
      Propositional logic assumes that there are facts that either hold or do  
      not hold  
      FOL assumes there are objects in the world, and there are relations  
      among them that do or do not hold  
    Temporal logic  
      Facts hold at particular times  
    Higher-order logic  
      Relations and functions are also objects  
4     More expressive than FOL
