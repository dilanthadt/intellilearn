### L10 – Uncertainty & Probability — page 28

Conditional probability  
●   Conditional or posterior probabilities  
     ○   e.g., P(cavity \| toothache) = 0.8  
     ○   i.e., given that toothache is all I know  
●   If we know more, e.g., there is a rough spot on the tooth, then we have  
     ○   P(cavity \| toothache, rough\_spot) = 0.9  
●   New evidence may be irrelevant, allowing simpliﬁcation, e.g.,  
     ○   P(cavity \| toothache, sunny) = P(cavity \| toothache) = 0.8  
●   This kind of inference, sanctioned by domain knowledge, is crucial
