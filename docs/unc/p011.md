### L10 – Uncertainty & Probability — page 11

Methods for handling uncertainty  
 ●    Default or non-monotonic logic\*:  
        ○    logical system where conclusions drawn can be retracted or revised based on new  
        information  
        ○    E.g., assume A25 works unless contradicted by evidence  
        ○    Issues: What assumptions are reasonable? How to handle contradiction?  
 ●    Probability  
        ○    E.g., Given the available evidence, A25 will get me there on time with probability 0.04  
  
  
  
  
\*You may assume, "Birds can ﬂy," and thus conclude "Tweety can ﬂy" if Tweety is a bird. But if new information is  
introduced (e.g., "Tweety is a penguin"), you would retract the conclusion that Tweety can ﬂy, as penguins are birds that  
cannot ﬂy
