### L10 – Uncertainty & Probability — page 15

Probability  
●   Probabilistic assertions summarize eﬀects of  
     ○   laziness: failure to enumerate exceptions, etc.  
     ○   ignorance: lack of relevant facts, initial conditions, etc.  
●   Subjective or Bayesian probability:  
     ○   Probabilities relate propositions to one's own state of knowledge  
     ○   e.g., P(A25 gets me there on time \| no reported accidents) = 0.06  
●   Probabilities of propositions change with new evidence:  
     ○   E.g., P(A25 gets me there on time \| no reported accidents, 5 a.m.) = 0.15
