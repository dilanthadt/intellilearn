### L10 – Uncertainty & Probability — page 19

Propositions  
●   Think of a proposition as the event (set of sample points) where the  
    statement denoted by the proposition is true  
     ○   Given the random variable, X= outcome of a roll of a dice  
●   The proposition, X\_is\_Odd , can be thought of as the event,  
     ○   X\_is\_Odd = { 𝞈 ∊ 𝝮 \| X is Odd}  
●   Proposition = disjunction of atomic events in which it is true. E.g.  
     ○   X\_is \_Odd ⇔ (X=1) V (X=3) V (X=5)  
     ○   (a V b) ⇔ ( ᆨa ⴷ b) (a ⴷ ᆨb) (a ⴷ b)
